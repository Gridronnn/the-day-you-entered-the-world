## 2. Status Definitions

- **`live`**: Real-time market feed observed within seconds or minutes (e.g., streaming spot rate with an active `GOLDAPI_KEY`).
- **`delayed`**: Verified official observation from an authorized provider with standard publication lag (e.g., daily ECB reference rates, weekly EIA gasoline surveys, monthly BLS CPI).
- **`historical`**: Static, verified observation recorded for a past date (e.g., LBMA PM fix in 1995, PPAC fuel price in 2002).
- **`modeled`**: Scientifically reconstructed dataset using validated meteorological or demographic models (e.g., Open-Meteo ERA5 atmospheric reanalysis, UN Population Prospects).
- **`calculated`**: Exact mathematical or astronomical derivation based on standardized algorithms (e.g., Meeus moon phase, Julian Day, inflation factor ratio).
- **`stale`**: Cached observation that could not be refreshed before its expected update schedule.
- **`unavailable`**: Provider key missing or archive has no coverage for the requested date, unit, or location.
- **`error`**: Provider endpoint failed or timed out.
